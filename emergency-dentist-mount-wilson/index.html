What urgent dental care can involve

An emergency dentist Mount Wilson residents are considering can help assess a sudden dental problem and discuss an appropriate next step. Examples include a broken or painful tooth, a lost filling, swelling, or an injury to the mouth. The right response depends on what happened and how severe the symptoms are, so a phone conversation is a useful place to start.

For severe swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, or bleeding that will not stop, seek emergency medical care. A dental office is not a substitute for emergency medical services.
